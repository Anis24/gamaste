Dino Catch

Point your phone at the world outside. The game reads the season and the landscape, places a dinosaur in the camera view, and you tap to catch it. Each catch turns into a sticker in your personal album.

Try it on the phone: tap the dinosaur or press Collect. Click any step below to jump to it.
